Model stability was assessed through two complementary analyses. Three-fold stratified cross-validation (stratification by CO2 uptake decile bins, n = 10) on the full 278,778-structure dataset, using n_estimators=300 as a conservative lower-bound stability test (reduces per-fold training time by approximately 70% relative to the primary n_estimators=900 model), yields CO2 uptake R2 = 0.978 +/- 0.000, working capacity R2 = 0.982 +/- 0.000, CO2/H2 selectivity R2 = 0.971 +/- 0.000 (log-space), and heat of adsorption R2 = 0.817 +/- 0.001 (Supplementary Table S1). The primary test-set values in Table 1 use the fully optimised n_estimators=900 model and are therefore slightly higher than these reduced-estimator CV means; fold-to-fold variance remains negligible either way, confirming results do not reflect a favourable random partition. Seed-stability analysis across three independent 90/10 random partitions (seeds 42, 0, 123) shows R2 variation of 0.00034 (CO2 uptake), 0.00033 (working capacity), 0.00016 (selectivity), and 0.00450 (heat of adsorption) -- all well below the 0.002 stability threshold (Supplementary Table S2).
