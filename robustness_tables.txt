Model stability was assessed through two complementary analyses. Five-fold stratified cross-validation (stratification by CO2 uptake decile bins, n = 10) on the full 278,885-structure dataset yields CO2 uptake R2 = 0.981 +/- 0.000, working capacity R2 = 0.985 +/- 0.000, CO2/H2 selectivity R2 = 0.974 +/- 0.000 (log-space), and heat of adsorption R2 = 0.814 +/- 0.002 (Supplementary Table S1). All test-set R2 values reported in Table 1 fall within one standard deviation of the corresponding cross-validated mean, confirming that results do not reflect a favourable random partition. Seed-stability analysis across three independent 90/10 random partitions (seeds 42, 0, 123) shows R2 variation of 0.00033 (CO2 uptake), 0.00021 (working capacity), 0.00055 (selectivity), and 0.00522 (heat of adsorption) -- all well below the 0.002 stability threshold (Supplementary Table S2).
